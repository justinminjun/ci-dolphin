3
Search the place & fill in a few details
In "Search the place on Google Maps…", type the spot's name and tap it — this auto-fills the name, address & location (and keeps duplicates out). Then pick a category, add a photo, and write a line about why you love it.
4
Tap Post — done!
The green Post button is in the top-right. That's it — your tip is live on the Local Guide and your name is in the raffle. 🏆
💡 One place, one post

Each place can only be recommended once. If someone has already shared your favorite spot, the app will let you know — just pick a different one (there's plenty of Songdo to go around!) so your entry still counts. Want better odds? A second spot earns a second entry.

If you already posted in the Lounge, no worries at all — just re-post it on the Local Guide using the steps above and you're set. Thank you again for sharing the places that make Songdo feel like home. 💙
